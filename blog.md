## Blog 1
Learning data in social and interdisciplinary context matters because it helps relate real-world issues. When studying data through social perspectives, you can see how it impacts people and society, making it more relevant and understandable. Interdisciplinary learning allows you to combine knowledge from other areas like science, economics, and technology, giving you a shorter but deeper understanding. This will help someone's critical thinking and ability to communicate through various places. It also helps prepare you to use data responsibly in complex environments. Some responsibilities are privacy protection so that your information is protected from any viruses and not shared without consent. Accuracy is also a responsibility because if your information is inaccurate, you will not be a trusted source and will receive bad reviews. One other responsibility would be about accountability. It is important to take accountability in your information and be able to explain it as it is your own work. These responsibilities help build trust and ensure data is used appropriately and safely. Through this course I am hoping to better understand how to properly read code and be able to set up functions correctly and understand why the code/function is the way it is. I also hope to have an updated portfolio that is ready to be looked at professionally. One final goal of mine in this class is to be able to communicate my work to others in a way that makes sense to me as well as my peers and/or audience.
